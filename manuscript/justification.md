# 100-Word Justification — Surface Topography: Metrology and Properties

This paper asks how much spatial information a single quantum probe can extract from a surface, and answers it in metrological terms.  The instrument transfer function $H_x \propto k_x^2 e^{-kh}$ of a trapped-ion surface probe imposes an information cutoff that quantum enhancement cannot circumvent, and a topography–charge identifiability theorem bounds what any non-contact electrostatic method can reconstruct.  The framework includes a GUM-based uncertainty budget, SI-traceable calibration, ISO~25178-6 classification considerations, and numerical evaluation on published AFM topography, providing a complete measurement-principle study for the areal surface-metrology readership of STMP.
